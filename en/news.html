news.html —— Qingchuan Daily · August-September 2003

2003-08-22 · Student from No.3 High reported missing

Lu Yuan, 17, class 3, Grade 2 at Qingchuan No.3 High School, has not been home since August 14. Family has reported to police. Investigation ongoing.

2003-09-03 · Police sweep old dock area

Police have searched the old dock district in the northwest of the city, visiting residents and abandoned buildings. No leads found so far.

2003-09-18 · Case listed as missing person

Police state the Lu Yuan case is being handled as a missing person case. It will be reopened if new leads emerge.

📌 KEY
After August 14, Lu Yuan never appeared again.
The old dock area was searched, but nothing was found.
