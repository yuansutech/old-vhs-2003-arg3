missing.html —— Qingchuan Police Department

Name: Lu Yuan

Sex: Male

Born: 1986

Missing since: August 14, 2003

Last seen: Lu Yuan's home, West District, Qingchuan

Status: UNRESOLVED

On the evening of August 14, 2003, Lu Yuan was last seen by a neighbor at home. He disappeared that night. His parents found the house empty the next day.

A Panasonic camcorder and one VHS tape (serial VHS-4417) were found in his room.

The tape contains footage from June to August 2003, including fragments where he mentions "a vehicle following him."

A silver van had been parked in the neighborhood for several days. It disappeared after August 15. No plate number was recorded.

Case remains open.
