// VHS-2003 · Home tape · Summer 2003

Before anything else

This tape was recorded with my family's old camcorder. A Panasonic, I think, from the 90s.

My name is Lu Yuan. I'm 17. Second year at Qingchuan No.3 High School. Nothing much to do over summer break, so I recorded whatever I felt like.

At first I just filmed the cat, the window, me doing homework. Then things changed.

After August 14, 2003, the tape just stops.

If you find it — please finish watching it for me.

I left the passwords in other places.

— Lu Yuan · August 14, 2003 19:42
